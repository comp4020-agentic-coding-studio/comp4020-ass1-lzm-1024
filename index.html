The Lag: military aviation leads, civil aviation follows

Civil aviation

  1. 1903 Wright Flyer

    First sustained, controlled, powered flight, at Kitty Hawk, North Carolina.

  2. 1914 St. Petersburg–Tampa Airboat Line

    A Benoist XIV flying boat flies the first scheduled passenger airline service, across Tampa Bay, Florida.

  3. 1919 KLM founded

    KLM is founded and is still operating today under its original name — the oldest airline in the world.

  4. 1927 Spirit of St. Louis

    Charles Lindbergh completes the first solo nonstop transatlantic flight, New York to Paris.

  5. 1933 Boeing 247

    Widely called the first modern airliner: all-metal monocoque construction, retractable gear, and an autopilot.

  6. 1935 Douglas DC-3

    Cuts per-seat operating costs enough that airlines can turn a profit carrying passengers alone, without mail subsidies.

  7. 1939 Boeing 314 Clipper

    Pan Am's flying boat opens the first regular passenger service across the North Atlantic.

  8. 1949 de Havilland Comet

    First flight of the world's first jet airliner.

  9. 1952 Comet enters service

    BOAC begins the first scheduled jet passenger service, eight years after the first jet fighter entered service.

  10. 1958 Boeing 707 enters service

    Jet airliners go mainstream on transatlantic routes.

  11. 1959 Sud Aviation Caravelle

    Enters service as the first airliner with rear-mounted jet engines, a layout still common today.

  12. 1969 Boeing 747 first flight

    The "Jumbo Jet" flies for the first time.

  13. 1970 747 enters service

    Pan Am puts the first widebody airliner into commercial service.

  14. 1972 Airbus A300 first flight

    The first Airbus, and the first twin-engine widebody airliner.

  15. 1976 Concorde enters service

    Air France and British Airways begin supersonic passenger service — 29 years after the sound barrier was first broken in the air.

  16. 1988 Airbus A320 enters service

    The first fly-by-wire airliner, 14 years after the first fly-by-wire fighter flew.

  17. 1994 Boeing 777 first flight

    The first airliner designed entirely with computer-aided design, and later the first twin-engine jet cleared for the longest overwater diversion routes.

  18. 2003 Concorde retired

    Supersonic passenger travel ends, with no successor since.

  19. 2005 Airbus A380 first flight

    The largest passenger airliner ever built enters flight test.

  20. 2009 Boeing 787 first flight

    The first airliner with a majority-composite airframe, 20 years after the first majority-composite bomber flew.

  21. 2020 COVID-19 grounds civil aviation

    Global passenger traffic collapses — the one node on this timeline where civil aviation itself, not the military, drives the story.

Fighter aircraft

  1. 1915 Fokker Eindecker

    The first purpose-built fighter with a machine gun synchronized to fire through the propeller.

  2. 1917 Sopwith Camel

    The top-scoring Allied fighter of the First World War.

  3. 1918 Fokker D.VII

    Widely regarded as the best fighter of the First World War.

  4. 1935 Messerschmitt Bf 109

    First flight of the fighter that would become the Luftwaffe's backbone.

  5. 1936 Supermarine Spitfire

    First flight of the fighter that would defend Britain's skies.

  6. 1940 Battle of Britain

    Spitfires and Hurricanes turn back the Luftwaffe — the first major campaign in history decided in the air.

  7. 1942 Vought F4U Corsair

    Enters service as one of the most capable carrier fighters of the Pacific war.

  8. 1944 Messerschmitt Me 262

    Enters service as the first operational jet fighter — eight years before any civil jet carried a paying passenger.

  9. 1947 Bell X-1

    Chuck Yeager becomes the first pilot to exceed the speed of sound in level flight.

  10. 1953 North American F-100 Super Sabre

    First fighter capable of supersonic speed in level flight to enter regular service.

  11. 1958 Lockheed F-104 Starfighter

    Enters service, built for climb and speed above all else.

  12. 1960 McDonnell F-4 Phantom II

    Enters service as one of the most widely produced Western jet fighters of the century.

  13. 1964 Mikoyan MiG-25

    First flight of an interceptor built to fly at more than three times the speed of sound.

  14. 1974 General Dynamics F-16

    First flight of the first production fighter with fly-by-wire flight controls.

  15. 1976 McDonnell Douglas F-15 Eagle

    Enters service as an air-superiority fighter built to outperform anything the Soviet Union was flying.

  16. 1977 Sukhoi Su-27

    First flight of the Soviet Union's answer to the F-15.

  17. 1991 Lockheed F-117 Nighthawk

    Flies its combat debut in the Gulf War — the first stealth aircraft used in combat.

  18. 1997 F-22 Raptor first flight

    First flight of what would become the first operational stealth air-superiority fighter.

  19. 2005 F-22 enters service

    The F-22 Raptor becomes operational — no civil aircraft has ever used stealth shaping.

  20. 2006 Lockheed Martin F-35

    First flight of a stealth fighter built in three variants for air forces, navies, and marine corps across a dozen countries.

  21. 2017 Chengdu J-20

    Enters service as China's first operational stealth fighter.

  22. 2020 Sukhoi Su-57

    Enters service as Russia's first operational stealth fighter.

Bomber aircraft

  1. 1913 Sikorsky Ilya Muromets

    First flight of one of the first aircraft purpose-built as a multi-engine bomber.

  2. 1935 Boeing B-17 Flying Fortress

    First flight of the bomber that would become a mainstay of the US Army Air Forces over Europe.

  3. 1939 Vickers Wellington

    Enters service as one of the RAF's principal early-war heavy bombers.

  4. 1942 Avro Lancaster

    Enters service as the RAF's primary heavy night bomber for the rest of the war.

  5. 1944 Boeing B-29 Superfortress

    Enters service as the first bomber with a pressurized crew cabin.

  6. 1945 Hiroshima and Nagasaki

    On 6 and 9 August 1945, the B-29s Enola Gay and Bockscar drop the first nuclear weapons used in war, on Hiroshima and Nagasaki. Publicly documented estimates put the deaths by the end of 1945 at roughly 140,000 in Hiroshima and 70,000 in Nagasaki.

  7. 1949 Convair B-36 Peacemaker

    Enters service as the first bomber able to reach intercontinental targets without refueling.

  8. 1952 Boeing B-52 Stratofortress

    Enters service — and, more than 70 years later, is still in active service today.

  9. 1956 Avro Vulcan

    Enters service as one of the RAF's delta-winged V-bombers of the Cold War.

  10. 1961 Convair B-58 Hustler

    Enters service as the first supersonic bomber — 15 years before any airliner reached supersonic passenger service.

  11. 1986 Rockwell B-1B Lancer

    Enters service as a long-range, swing-wing strategic bomber.

  12. 1987 Tupolev Tu-160

    Enters service as the Soviet Union's supersonic strategic bomber.

  13. 1989 Northrop B-2 Spirit first flight

    First flight of the first flying-wing stealth bomber, with a majority-composite airframe.

  14. 1997 B-2 enters service

    The B-2 Spirit becomes the first operational stealth bomber.

  15. 2023 Northrop B-21 Raider

    First flight of the newest US stealth bomber, designed to eventually replace the B-1B and B-2.
