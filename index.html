The part most drivers underestimate

Speed costs you twice.

Reaction distance rises with speed. Braking distance rises with speed squared—and wet roads or worn tread stretch it further.

Evidence, not a guarantee

What this model assumes

Reaction time is fixed at 1.5 seconds. Dry and wet braking are calibrated to the Queensland Government stopping-distance table.

Wet tread adjustment uses Continental tests at 8.0, 3.0 and 1.6mm. Dry tread adjustment uses a US Tire Rack test at approximately 8.0, 3.2 and 1.6mm. Values between test points are linear interpolations.

The dry test found only a 1.7% shorter stop with shallower tread. It does not show that aged, cracked or damaged tyres are safer, and results vary by tyre and vehicle.
